## BEST FREE TOOLS (ranked for photorealistic food)

1. **Google ImageFX** — labs.google/fx/tools/image-fx · free with a Google account,
   no watermark. Runs Imagen — the most photoreal of the free tools for food. **Use this first.**
   Tip: set the aspect ratio in the tool, keep the word "photograph" in the prompt,
   generate 4, pick the most natural (avoid the over-shiny one).

2. **Bing Image Creator** — bing.com/images/create · free with a Microsoft account,
   runs DALL·E 3. Follows long detailed prompts very well; excellent plating realism.
   Tip: it auto-enhances, so trust the prompt as written; regenerate if a tiny watermark appears.

3. **Leonardo.ai** — leonardo.ai · ~150 free tokens/day. Best when you want control.
   Tip: pick model **"Leonardo Phoenix"** or **"Lucid Realism"**, turn ON PhotoReal/Alchemy,
   set aspect ratio, then paste the prompt.

4. **Adobe Firefly** — firefly.adobe.com · free monthly credits, commercially safe.
   Good, slightly softer food look. Set "Content type: Photo".

5. **Krea.ai** — krea.ai · free tier, Flux-based, fast and very realistic.

> For the most consistent match to your two existing shots, do all 5 in the **same tool**
> (ImageFX recommended) so the lighting and colour grade line up.
