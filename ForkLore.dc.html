Our story

The story your body needs

ForkLore did not start as a food company. It started as an argument about how much of your day nutrition is allowed to take.

chapter one

Why we started

Everyone we knew who was trying to get fitter was spending an absurd amount of attention on food. Not cooking it — deciding about it. Reading labels, rebuilding the same plate, guessing at portions, then losing the thread by Thursday.

The food wasn't the problem. The number of decisions was.

chapter two

What is broken

Healthy food in India is usually sold in one of two forms. Either it is delicious and unmeasured, or it is measured and joyless — a bowl of something Western that nobody in the family recognises.

And almost nowhere does the number on the label survive contact with the kitchen. Portions drift. Recipes drift. Trust goes with them.

Delicious but unmeasuredno numbers
Measured but foreignnobody sticks to it
Planned but unsupportedweek three, gone
chapter three

What we believe

01

Nutrition should cost you attention once, not daily.

02

A number you can't verify is decoration.

03

Food you grew up with is the only food you'll keep eating.

chapter four

What we are building

Not a menu with a health claim attached. A system: a goal, a plan derived from it, fixed recipes cooked to that plan, stated nutrition, and a twelve-week arc that expects real life to interfere.

Where we are

Early. Building the kitchen system before the marketing.
